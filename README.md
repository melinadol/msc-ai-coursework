# MSc in Artificial Intelligence & Visual Computing: coursework

Projects and exercises from the **MSc in Artificial Intelligence & Visual Computing**, University of West Attica (2022–2024), by **Maria Melina Dolapsaki**.

| Area | Project | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| **Big Data** | [Hotel review sentiment analysis](big-data-hotel-reviews/) (2023) | NLP on 515K Booking.com reviews: NLTK cleaning, VADER sentiment, Doc2Vec & TF-IDF, Random Forest (ROC AUC 0.86), cosine-similarity search, co-occurrence graphs |
| **Deep Learning** | [Classifiers, CNN vs DNN, RNN/LSTM/GRU](deep-learning/) (2023) | bankruptcy prediction with 8 classifiers on imbalanced data; MNIST CNN 97.1 % vs DNN 95.8 % (6-fold CV); appliance energy disaggregation with RNN/LSTM/GRU (GRU best) |
| **Machine Learning** | [LVQ2 & LFM](machine-learning/lvq2-lfm/) | prototype-based classifiers implemented from scratch in MATLAB (Iris, Wine) |
| **Image Processing** | [Otsu & local thresholding](image-processing/otsu-local-thresholding/) (2022) | Otsu's method and mean/median adaptive thresholding from scratch (MATLAB) |
| **3D Data Processing** | [Laser-scan registration with ICP](3d-data-processing/laser-scan-registration-icp/) | TLS scan cleaning, normals, ICP and global alignment in MeshLab (avg. error 0.0045), meshing |
| **Fuzzy Systems** | coming soon | |
